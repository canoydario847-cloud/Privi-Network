## What problem are we solving?

When a small software team deploys a release, a green build check alone does not establish that the intended version is running or that agreed functional checks returned expected responses. People responsible for the release still need an understandable, limited evidence trail they can review.

## What have we built?

Privi DONE has a working **company-owned prelaunch sandbox** at https://privi-done-version-preview.onrender.com and an internal workflow that compares a pinned source commit with a service's reported version, health response, local audit and unsigned checkpoint response. An external GitHub-hosted observer successfully fetched these four fixed GET endpoints and the pilot-review logic passed 67 tests at its initial review milestone. These results are not proof of independent cryptographic execution attestation, customer acceptance or verified customer savings.

Public pilot intake: https://github.com/canoydario847-cloud/Privi-Network/issues/new?template=privi-done-free-pilot.yml

## Current stage

**Prelaunch and customer discovery.** A functioning internal demonstration and a published pilot-interest form exist. We have not established external pilot acceptance, repeatable paying-customer demand, or certified completion of an external customer's deployment. No customer billing is part of the initial pilot.

## Target customer

Small software teams, AI-automation integrators, developer consultancies and startup founders who manage staging releases and need a clear, human-readable review of release identity, CI check status and pre-agreed functional behaviors.

## Our initial free pilot

With one consenting deployment owner, agree in writing on a non-critical staging release, a pinned commit, required checks and fixed read-only endpoints. Review allowed evidence, document gaps and limitations, and ask an authorized human representative to accept or reject the report. No production credentials, private data, financial token transfers or automatic charges.
