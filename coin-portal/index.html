05 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I buy PRVI right now? +

No. PRVI's proposed financial mainnet is not active and no verified primary sale, exchange listing or purchase contract is available. This portal does not accept funds.

What is the proposed supply? +

The current technical specification proposes a lifetime maximum of 1,000,000,000 native PRVI. This supply has not been minted, and production enforcement has not been independently audited.

Why can I check my wallet but not buy? +

The wallet checker only reads the currently selected address and EVM chain ID from an installed wallet if you approve the connection. It does not sign messages, authorize token spending, or submit transactions.

Are PRVI and BNB PRIV the same? +

No. The proposed future native PRVI, valueless developer TEST/atest, and separate BNB PRIV are different assets. No swap or migration is promised here.

How will I know when buying is safe? +

Wait for independently verifiable network details, an audited transaction path, official announcements and clearly documented market and legal availability. Never send funds to an address advertised informally as a PRVI sale.
