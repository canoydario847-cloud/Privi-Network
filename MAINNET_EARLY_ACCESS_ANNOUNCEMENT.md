# Privi Mainnet — Join the early-access community

**Privi World Corp has launched its public mainnet pre-launch website:**

**https://privi-mainnet-portal-live.onrender.com**

The portal is now available for people interested in the future Privi network.

## What visitors can do today

- Learn about Privi's planned native network and PRVI token.
- Connect an existing EVM wallet **read-only** to preview the wallet interface. No wallet signing or transfer is required.
- Preview proposed staking features; actual PRVI staking is **not active**.
- Request early-access and launch updates via a prepared email to Privi World Corp (visitors must manually send it).
- Check the launch section. A countdown will appear when an official launch date has been verified and announced.

## Safety and launch status

**The financial mainnet has not been verified live. No native PRVI deposits, transfers, claiming, or staking are offered through this site.** Do not send tokens or share your recovery phrase with anyone claiming to offer early access. The portal does not guarantee token allocations, rewards, profits, or eligibility.
