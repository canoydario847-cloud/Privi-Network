# Privi World for Android

Trusted Web Activity that opens the Privi World arcade (`https://privi-world.onrender.com/world/`) full screen in
Chrome, where camera AR, the creature duel and the map work as on the web.

- Package: `us.priviflow.world`. Signed with the same upload key as Privi RealCheck (GitHub secrets
  `REALCHECK_KEYSTORE_B64` / `REALCHECK_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`).
- Digital Asset Links: `sites/home/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (add the Play app-signing SHA-256 after the first upload).
- Built by `.github/workflows/android-world.yml`.

## Suggested listing
**Title:** Privi World: AR Adventures
**Short:** Turn the places around you into silly AR adventures and creature duels.
**Full:** Catch flying avocados, alien photobombers and pizza meteors through your phone camera, design your own creature
and battle the Giggle Goblin, and pick a real-world spot on the map as your arena. Includes a split-screen preview for
phone VR headsets. Play only in safe, open spaces; never while walking or driving. Content rating: Everyone; camera and
optional location are used only on the device.
