# Privi enterprise AI-agent evidence pilot

Privi World Corp | https://privi-hub.onrender.com

## The problem
Enterprise teams need inspectable records of what an AI agent was authorized to do, which policy applied, and what execution evidence actually exists. An audit log alone does not prove an action happened.

## Product components to evaluate
- Privi Guard: agent-input and tool-use safety screening.
- Privi Ledger: tamper-evident event records.
- Privi Verify: exported record verification.
- Privi Quantum: research and experimental hybrid security controls; no claim of production post-quantum guarantees.

## Proposed two-week pilot (subject to technical scoping)
1. Choose one non-sensitive AI-agent workflow.
2. Record actor, policy version, requested action, approval decision and software version.
3. Bind execution outcome to a signed/exportable receipt where implementation supports it; clearly label missing evidence.
4. Demonstrate independent verification, tampering rejection, and fail-closed authorization checks.
5. Deliver an evidence matrix and integration report.
