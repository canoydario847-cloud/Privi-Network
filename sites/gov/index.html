Post-quantum readiness package

Free for agencies and contractors through the Privi Quantum MCP server (pqc_readiness_package) or the demo page.

Live check of your public sites
Enter up to 20 hostnames, such as www.agency.gov. Privi Quantum tests each one for TLS 1.3 and hybrid post-quantum key exchange (X25519MLKEM768, FIPS 203), the protection against “harvest now, decrypt later,” and adds the results to your CBOM. It sends two TLS hellos per site and no data.
Inventory in, plan out
Scan code and configuration, add your systems' algorithms, protocols, FIPS 199 impact and HVA status. Every asset gets the federal deadline that applies to it and a FIPS 203/204/205 replacement.
The nine M-26-15 plan elements
Each element is marked as evidence in the package or as a gap the agency must supply, so the living migration plan you submitted on October 22 keeps improving.
CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM
A machine-readable Cryptographic Bill of Materials of every asset, ready for the minimum CBOM elements CISA and NIST are due to publish in March 2027.
Signed and anchored
Each package is signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204, the CNSA 2.0 parameter set) and its hash is anchored on chain (Base today; the Privi chain once its mainnet launches). Anyone can check it on its public report page.
Contractors and NSS vendors
Contractor profile: EO 14409 FAR items (FIPS incl. PQC by Dec 31, 2030, proposed). NSS vendor profile: NSA CNSA 2.0 dates per product category.

AI use-case inventories and continuous records

AI use-case inventory
Records in OMB M-25-21 reporting fields. Every change is a new version in the tamper-evident log, and a readiness check flags missing fields, PII without a privacy impact assessment and high-impact AI missing minimum risk practices.
Versioned crypto inventory
Privi Ledger keeps each system's cryptographic inventory as a versioned record and exports the CBOM and migration plan sealed on chain.
Two-person integrity
Require two officers' signatures before an agent changes the inventory, publishes an export or takes any other sensitive step.
Evidence for oversight
Hand an inspector a dual-signed bundle they can verify on an air-gapped machine.

Plain about certification

Privi is an early-stage company. It does not yet hold FedRAMP authorization, a DoD Impact Level or FIPS 140-3 module validation, and its deadlines follow EO 14409, OMB M-26-15, the NIST IR 8547 draft and CNSA 2.0 as of October 2026. Pilots with agencies and contractors are welcome.
