Let agents spend. Within limits a person signed.

When an agent pays on someone's behalf, the merchant needs to know a human agreed. A Privi Mandate is that agreement: plain-English terms the person signs once with their wallet, enforced on every payment and checkable by anyone for free.

How it worksUse it in your agent

A mandate says

The exact text a person signs.

I authorize ShopBot's agent 'shopper' to spend up to 100 USDC in total, at most 40 USDC per payment, to only these payees: store.example.com, until 2026-11-20 UTC.
Purpose: Buy office supplies.
I can revoke this at any time.

How it works

  1. The agent drafts terms

    Budget, per-payment cap, allowed payees by address or domain, expiry and purpose. Privi returns a signing link.

  2. The person signs once

    They read the terms in plain English and sign with their wallet. Privi countersigns and anchors the terms on chain.

  3. Every payment is checked

    Before paying, the agent records the spend. Over-cap, wrong-payee, expired or revoked payments are refused and logged as blocked.

  4. Merchants verify for free

    A merchant or facilitator checks the mandate and gets a signed allow or deny with the remaining budget.

Check a mandate

The person can revoke from the same page they signed on, with one more signature. Revocation takes effect immediately for every checker.

GET https://privi-ledger.onrender.com/mandates/<mandate_id>/check?amount=25&payee=store.example.com
