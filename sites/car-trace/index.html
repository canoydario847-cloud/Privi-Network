Privi Car and Privi Trace

Privi Car lets a parked car do useful computing work while the owner stays in control, with no access to driving systems. Privi Trace keeps a sealed, step-by-step record of where something came from: a part, a stablecoin payment, an AI agent's actions, or a car's history.

Product 1

Privi Car

A small AI helper that runs on a companion device next to the car, or on compute the carmaker has approved. It only works when the owner has opted in and the car is parked, plugged in and cool. It can never touch steering, brakes, firmware or the car's internal network.

The owner decides

Every permission starts switched off. The owner turns on what they want and can switch it all off at any time. The car keeps working normally when the helper is disconnected.

Four checks before any job

Opted in, parked, on external power, safe temperature. If any check fails, the job is refused. Nothing runs while driving.

A receipt for every job

Each finished job produces a signed receipt: what ran, on which device (by pseudonym, not VIN), how much power and time it used, and a hash of the result. Rewards in the prototype are test credits that cannot be transferred.

A sealed vehicle passport

Service visits, ownership changes and digital keys go into a passport that can't be quietly edited later. That passport also feeds Privi Trace.

    Product 2

    Privi Trace

    A chain of signed checkpoints. Each checkpoint records who did what and when, and is sealed together with the one before it. Change any earlier step and every seal after it stops matching, so anyone can see exactly where a record was altered.

    Parts and materials

    Follow a lot from the steel mill to heat treat, machining, inspection and shipment. Built on Privi Supplier Proof.

    Stablecoin payments

    Check the issuer's status and its latest sealed reserve report, then record the transfer and the receipt. Built on the GENIUS Act tools.

    AI agent actions

    A replayable trail of what an agent read, which tools it called, what Guard flagged, and who approved the result. Built on Privi Ledger Pro.

    Vehicle history

    Owners, service, mileage and digital keys from the Privi Car passport, in one record a buyer can verify.

      Privi Pro for your car

      Pro turns the car into its own small computer with its own records: pay at the pump from the dashboard, keep a car wallet, earn rewards for verified compute jobs, and carry your own offline maps and ledger. These features are only for Privi Pro accounts.

      Pay at the pump from the dashboard

      Pick a Privi gas station and a pump, choose an amount, and confirm with Face ID or a fingerprint on your phone. Payment only works when the car is parked with the engine off. The receipt is sealed into the car's ledger.

      Car wallet

      A USDC wallet tied to the car, with every fuel payment and reward listed. The owner approves each payment; the car never spends on its own.

      Compute rewards

      When the car runs a verified compute or storage job while parked, it earns reward credits. In this preview they are test credits that can't be transferred or cashed out. Paid rewards come later, from a funded pool, after legal review. No coins are created or mined.

      The car is its own database

      The car keeps its ledger, passport and receipts on board and anchors them on Base when it has a connection. Offline maps live on the car too, so navigation and records keep working with no signal.

          How sealing works

          Step 1

          Record the event

          Who acted, what happened, when, and the key data, such as a certificate number, a mileage reading or a transaction hash.

          Step 2

          Seal it to the last one

          Hash the event together with the previous seal. The new seal depends on the entire history before it.

          Step 3

          Anchor and share

          Checkpoints are anchored on Base, and anyone with the record can recompute every seal to confirm nothing changed.
