First five minutes

  1. Sign up from the agent

    Call ledger_signup with your company name. The response contains your organization key, shown once.

  2. Send the key

    Every request carries Authorization: Bearer <key>. The free plan includes 500 entries a month.

  3. Record actions

    Call ledger_record_action after each tool call, payment, message or decision. Keep the returned entry hash as a receipt.

  4. Prove it

    Issue a passport, export an evidence bundle or hand anyone an inclusion proof.
