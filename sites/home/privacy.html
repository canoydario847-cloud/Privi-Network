Privacy policy

Privi World Co · Effective October 25, 2026

This policy covers the Privi app for Android and the Privi website at privi-world.onrender.com and priviflow.us. Privi World Co is a Delaware corporation based in Port Huron, Michigan.

What the app does

The Privi app opens the Privi website in your phone's Chrome browser. The app itself does not collect, store or share any personal information, and it asks for no device permissions.

What the website loads

  • Fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load.
  • A live status check from Privi Ledger, which shows the latest public log entry. It sends no information about you.
  • The website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

When you use Privi services

Some pages link to Privi services such as Ledger, Verify, Passport and Call. If you use them, they keep what you send them so they can do their job:

  • Organization name and API key if you sign up.
  • Records you choose to log, such as audit entries, lot records or call records.
  • Payment details recorded on the Base blockchain if you pay for a tool. Blockchain records are public and permanent.
  • If you call the Privi phone line, the call record and any message you leave. Public call logs show phone numbers only in masked or hashed form.

Some proofs are anchored on a public blockchain as hashes. A hash does not reveal the content it came from.

Sharing

Privi does not sell your information. It shares information only with the services that run Privi (hosting by Render, payments through Coinbase on Base, phone calls through Twilio) or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what Privi holds about you, or ask Privi to delete it, by emailing dariocanoy@priviflow.us. Records already anchored on a public blockchain cannot be removed from it.

Children

Privi is a business service and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
