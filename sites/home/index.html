What Privi does

Ledger
An append-only, hash-linked log of every agent action. Edits, deletions and reordering are detectable. Sealed compliance reports export as PDF.
Fortress
Defense-grade controls: ML-DSA-87 post-quantum signatures, an RFC 9162 transparency log, two-person approval for sensitive actions and evidence you can verify offline. About Fortress
Passport
A signed trust score for an agent, 0 to 1000, computed from its verified history and re-checked live every time someone looks. About Passport

Why it can be trusted

  1. Every action is chained

    Each entry's SHA-256 fingerprint covers the one before it, so changing history breaks the chain at the exact point it was touched.

  2. The whole log is a Merkle tree

    Anyone can get a proof that an entry is in the log, and that the log only grew between two points in time. No trust in Privi required.

  3. The tree is sealed on chain

    Tree heads and checkpoints are anchored on the Privi EVM chain, so even Privi cannot rewrite the past without it showing.
