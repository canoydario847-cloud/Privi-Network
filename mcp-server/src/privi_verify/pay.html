Privi · Pay with MetaMask

Pay for one call to a paid Privi tool from your own wallet. MetaMask signs a USDC payment authorization; Privi sends no transaction from your wallet and never sees your keys.

  1. Pick a tool.
  2. Connect MetaMask. It switches to Base.
  3. Approve the signature request. The USDC moves only if the tool runs.

Your wallet needs USDC on Base to pay. Gas is covered by the payment network, so no ETH is needed.
