Privi Voice for ChatGPT

Privi Voice is a free read-aloud player by Privi World Corp. Open it inside ChatGPT, choose an installed device voice and reading speed, then press Play. No Privi account or payment is required.

Privacy

If you ask ChatGPT to open Privi Voice with a reply or excerpt, ChatGPT sends that text to Privi’s hosted service so it can populate the player. The service handles the text for that request and does not save it or use it for advertising. Privi Voice has no access to your ChatGPT account or conversation history.

You can also open the player empty and paste text directly into it. Pasted text and playback settings stay in the player during use. Speech runs on your device using a local voice, and Privi Voice does not send text to a speech provider. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request metadata, such as IP addresses, to deliver the service. ChatGPT handles conversations according to its own privacy settings.
