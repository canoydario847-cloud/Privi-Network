# Privi Mainnet — request for independent validator hosting quotes

**PROCUREMENT PREPARATION ONLY. Do not order infrastructure or activate PRVI before the audited release is finalized.** This is a draft ready for customized outreach once technically viable; no provider has been contracted.

## Subject

Request for quote: independently operated Cosmos EVM validator and sentry for proposed Privi PRVI mainnet

## Message to potential infrastructure operator

Hello,

We are assembling an independently operated validator set for a proposed new Cosmos EVM proof-of-stake blockchain, **Privi Mainnet**, with proposed native coin ticker **PRVI**. We expect to commission a separately audited Privi-customized Cosmos EVM release, signed genesis and staking/fee configuration. This is NOT the current public valueless TEST development chain and is not connected to the existing PRIV token on BNB Chain.

We are seeking operators who will control their own signing keys and infrastructure, with one actual independent controlling entity per validator slot. We are starting with a target of at least **seven independent operators**, across at least three providers and regions. We are **not** promising a token price, staking yield, or a validator position before due diligence.

Please provide a nonbinding written quote for:

1. 24/7 operated CometBFT/Cosmos EVM validator with independent signing key custody; separate sentry and RPC architecture where needed
2. Your experience operating third-party Cosmos SDK or Cosmos EVM chains, relevant public references, and any limitations supporting a new custom binary
3. Dedicated managed VM/hardware, persistent storage, bandwidth, backup strategy, dedicated public P2P sentry, on-call support, monitoring and security response
4. Proposed infrastructure provider(s), region(s), DDoS and firewall safeguards; how operational control remains independent of all other validator applicants
5. Signing key custody, HSM/remote signer options, no-double-sign failover, encrypted backup and incident-recovery evidence
6. Upfront setup fee, recurring cost in USD, included storage and traffic, optional audit/onboarding fees, SLA, renewal and termination terms
7. Minimum lead time to verify a signed node binary, participate in a public-key-only genesis ceremony, and join a **valueless** operational rehearsal
8. Required payment method and invoicing / tax entity information. No payment information or credentials should be included in the first technical application
9. Your willingness to supply a public validator identity and an Ed25519 **separate operator attestation public key** to independently sign the final genesis and binary hashes

We will distribute the final audited software/release hashes and genesis separately after security and legal review. Please do not request developer or user wallet seed phrases or assume our TEST coins have financial value.

Thank you.

Privi Mainnet Project
