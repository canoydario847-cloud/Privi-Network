## Target channels and sequence

**Phase A — Before trading:** Kraken and Coinbase have official review/application paths; projects may prepare or apply for consideration, subject to review. Binance has a founder/team-led application process. **Application is not approval**. Submit through official forms only, after factual disclosures are complete.

**Phase B — After legitimate trading on supported markets:** CoinGecko's normal Active listing expects trading on a tracked exchange, working explorer, and a project-owned informative website. CoinMarketCap evaluates independent evidence and expects online-form submissions. CoinGecko Preview requires an announced, verifiable TGE date; none should be fabricated.

**Phase C — Venue expansion:** Each additional CEX or DEX requires jurisdiction, custody/chain support, integration, counterparty and liquidity checks. No one can guarantee listings on *all* markets, set listing decisions, or manufacture a trading price.
