## Release and marketing rules

1. Demonstrate a live, functioning server endpoint and honest functionality
   before directory submission; staging demos must be labeled as demos.
2. Do not list LIFE AI Studio as an MCP server until it actually implements MCP.
   Privi's other MCP products may qualify separately.
3. For Glama, check if the server is already indexed and claim ownership
   instead of creating duplicate listings; protect credentials and follow
   official domain/GitHub verification.
4. For the official MCP Registry, the namespace owner must authenticate before
   publication. A server.json file alone is not a successful listing.
5. For Product Hunt, verify users can sign in safely, personal data is durable
   and backed up, public support and privacy policies exist, and public
   accounts have verified identity and recovery.
6. Never promise or pay for placements without separate budget approval.
   Measure referral signups, activated developer keys, paying customers and
   campaign costs rather than impressions alone.
