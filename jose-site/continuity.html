Your computer should know how to survive.

Explore file integrity and verified recovery. This preview runs in your browser. It does not scan your operating system or send file contents to a server.

01 / Create a recovery snapshot

Choose a file up to 20 MiB, or try a harmless sample. The original bytes and SHA-256 fingerprint stay in this tab's memory.

02 / Detect a change

Choose another version to compare, or simulate a change to a copy of your snapshot.

03 / Verify and recover

Ready. No snapshot captured.Keep this tab open. Snapshots are temporary, not durable backups; downloaded files are not automatically encrypted.

04 / Encrypted portable recovery vault

Save an encrypted snapshot to your device, then import it in a later session. The password is never transmitted or stored. Use a strong, unique passphrase of at least 12 characters; losing it means losing access.



AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 iterations), random salt and nonce. Save a separate copy of the encrypted file. This preview has not undergone external security review.
