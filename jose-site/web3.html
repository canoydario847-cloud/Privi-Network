    What this launch flow does
    1. Generate Solidity and inspect it.
    2. Compile with Remix and paste the creation bytecode.
    3. Connect your wallet and choose a supported EVM network.
    4. Review estimated gas and explicitly approve in your wallet.
    5. Save the transaction hash and contract address.
    

    A new ERC-20 token contract is not an independently operated blockchain. The blockchain blueprint above is a separate infrastructure project, not a live network.
