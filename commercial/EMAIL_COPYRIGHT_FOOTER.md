# Standard Privi World Corp email copyright footer

Use this footer for business emails drafted or sent on behalf of Privi World Corp, including grant applications, partnership proposals, customer follow-ups, contracts discussions, marketing outreach, and technical proposals. Keep the wording unchanged unless the sender explicitly requests a different notice.

---

© 2026 Privi World Corp. All rights reserved.
Privi™, Privi World™, Privi CallProof™, and original Privi business materials are identified as property of Privi World Corp, subject to applicable ownership and third-party rights.
This email and its original Privi-authored attachments are provided for the intended business purpose only. No license to reproduce, redistribute, or commercially exploit proprietary Privi materials is granted without written authorization.

---

**Accuracy:** A copyright or property notice does not override licenses already granted (including MIT-licensed source), cannot claim ownership of a recipient's material, and does not make an email confidential or legally privileged. Do not label open-source content as proprietary. Preserve original third-party ownership, attributions and licenses. Do not automatically attach legal threats or prohibit ordinary necessary forwarding of sales emails. A footer does not prevent copying, forwarding, or screenshots.
